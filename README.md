# Art Exchange App

**Art Exchange** is an innovative app that allows creators to exchange their own art, designs, illustrations, DIY goods, and more with other users in a fun and creative community. The app promotes artistic exchange, fosters creativity, and helps build meaningful connections between creators around the world.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Young Creators (20s–30s):** Artists, designers, illustrators, and DIY enthusiasts who want to trade their work for exciting, one-of-a-kind items.  
- **Students & Emerging Artists:** Young professionals or students on a tight budget who want access to fresh creative materials and artwork.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Personalized Art Gallery:** Users can create their own gallery to showcase their work and explore others’ creations.  
- **Easy Exchange Requests:** A seamless and intuitive trading process where users can send and receive exchange requests based on preferences and saved lists.  
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Suggestions based on users’ interests and browsing behavior.  
- **Custom Saved Lists:** Users can organize and categorize their favorite artworks into custom collections.

## 🛠 Design Tools & Process
- **Design Tools:** Figma, Illustrator  
- **Design Approach:** The app features a clean, minimalist interface designed to enhance user experience. I used Figma for wireframing, prototyping, and UI design, and Illustrator for logo creation. The design prioritizes clarity and ease of use, allowing creators to focus on their artwork and exchanges.

## 🌟 Project Highlights
- **User-Centered Design:** Intuitive navigation and a visually engaging interface tailored to real user needs.  
- **Customizable Profiles:** Users can personalize their profiles and showcase their art in individual galleries.  
- **Trust & Transparency:** A built-in feedback system encourages a safe, supportive, and reliable creative community.

## 🧰 Tools Used
